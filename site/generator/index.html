How do you run now?

Not sure? Leave it: the plan is gentle when your long run is short or there is no race time.

Your race or goal

A race, or a goal of your own, such as 10 km by a date. New to running and no goal yet? Remove it: the plan then builds you up to 5 km in 9 weeks.

A recent race

Your paces are worked out from it. No race yet? Run 5 km on your own, as fast as you comfortably can, and enter that. New to running? Leave it empty and run at a pace where you can still talk.

Set the paces yourself

Any pace you give replaces the one worked out from the race, in minutes and seconds per km. Marathon pace is the place for a goal pace.

Change single weeks

A change affects only its own week; the weeks after it are still worked out from the rules. A week that holds a race keeps its race.
